About Sahel | Motion Designer & Video Editor

I'm Sahel, a Motion Designer and Video Editor focused on turning ideas into engaging visual stories. I combine video editing, motion graphics, and visual storytelling to create polished content for brands, creators, and businesses. My approach is to understand the message, shape the story, and use editing and motion to communicate it clearly.
